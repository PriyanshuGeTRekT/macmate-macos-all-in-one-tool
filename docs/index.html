Battery

See exactly which app is draining your battery.

The battery percentage lives in your menu bar. One click shows every app’s energy use in real watts, read from the chip’s own per-process counters, not a vague “energy impact” score.

  • Percent per hour each app costs, and the minutes of battery you get back by quitting it.
  • Battery history: 24-hour and 7-day charts of charge and of the apps that used the most energy.
  • An alert when an app suddenly starts using a lot of energy.
  • Battery levels of AirPods, mice and keyboards connected over Bluetooth.
  • Health, cycle count, adapter wattage and an optional unplug-at-80% reminder.

Windows

A window manager and Alt-Tab, built in.

Snap windows into place the way Windows does, with a shortcut or by dragging them to an edge. Switch between individual windows, not just apps.

  • 15 layouts: halves, quarters, thirds, two-thirds, maximize and center.
  • Drag a window to a screen edge or corner to snap it, with a live preview.
  • ⌥Tab switcher with a thumbnail of every window.
  • Window previews when you hover over an icon in the Dock.
  • Restore the previous size, move to the next display, optional gaps.

Mouse & keyboard

Your mouse and keyboard, the way you like them.

Reverse the scroll wheel of a third-party mouse while the trackpad keeps natural scrolling. Turn Caps Lock into something useful.

  • Reverse scrolling for the mouse only, smooth scrolling and adjustable speed.
  • Remap the middle, back and forward buttons to Mission Control, Spaces, Copy, Paste and more.
  • Caps Lock as a Hyper key, plus key remapping.
  • Custom keyboard shortcuts for MacMate’s actions.
  • The trackpad and Magic Mouse are never touched.

Sound & displays

A volume for every app. Brightness for every screen.

Turn down a noisy browser tab without touching your music, and switch speakers or headphones in one click.

  • Per-app volume for every app that plays audio.
  • Output-device switching right from the menu bar.
  • Hardware brightness for external monitors over DDC.
  • Brightness keys that work on external monitors too.

Calendar

Never be late for the next meeting.

See what is next at a glance and join video calls with one click.

  • Your next meeting at the top of the Calendar tab.
  • Join buttons for video calls.
  • An optional countdown to the next meeting in the menu bar.

Activity

Your whole Mac, at a glance.

A live system monitor in the menu bar, without opening Activity Monitor.

  • CPU history and per-core bars for efficiency and performance cores.
  • Memory pressure, swap, network speed, GPU, storage and thermal state.
  • Top processes by CPU or memory, with right-click to quit.

Keep Awake

Keep your Mac awake. For as long as you need.

From 15 minutes to 8 hours, a custom time or until you turn it off. Keep the display on or just the system, and stop automatically when the battery runs low.

Clipboard

Everything you copied, one shortcut away.

The last 100 text items with search and pinning. Press ⌃⌥V anywhere and click to paste. Kept in memory only, and passwords are never recorded.

Cleanup

Uninstall Mac apps completely.

Dragging an app to the Trash leaves its caches, preferences and support files behind. MacMate finds them all, shows you every file, and moves only what you tick to the Trash.

  • Leftovers of apps you have already deleted.
  • A finder for large and old files.
  • A manager for startup items and launch agents.
  • A junk cleaner for caches, logs and developer files.
  • Everything goes to the Trash, never deleted outright.

Tools

The little things macOS forgot.

Small helpers you would otherwise install one app at a time.

  • Copy text from anywhere on screen, even from images, videos and PDFs.
  • Color picker that copies the hex value of any pixel.
  • Global microphone mute with a hotkey.
  • Hold ⌘Q to quit, and cut and paste files in Finder.
  • Keyboard cleaning lock, display dimming and quick system toggles.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your Mac.

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. MacMate checks for updates and installs them for you, and that check is the only network request it ever makes.

  • Clipboard history lives in memory and is gone when the app quits.
  • Cleaners can only touch app data in known folders, and use the Trash.
  • Choose what the menu bar shows: time left, watts, CPU or network speed.
  • The source is small and readable. Check it yourself.

Replaces these apps.

One menu bar icon instead of a dozen. MacMate warns you if a conflicting app is running at the same time.

Up and running in a minute.

Requires macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.

  1. Download

    Download MacMate.zip, unzip it and drag MacMate.app into your Applications folder.

    Or install it with Homebrew:

    brew install --cask priyanshugetrekt/macmate/macmate

    Run the brew tap command shown at the top of the page first.

  2. Open Anyway

    MacMate is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to MacMate.

    You only need to do this once.

  3. Allow Accessibility

    Window tiling, ⌥Tab, mouse and keyboard features need Accessibility access. Click Open Privacy & Security Settings in MacMate and switch it on.

    Battery, Activity, Keep Awake, Clipboard and the cleaners work without any permission.

Prefer to build it yourself? Build from source with just the Command Line Tools.

Questions and answers.

How do I show battery percentage in the Mac menu bar?

Install MacMate. The percentage is always visible in the menu bar, and in Settings you can add time remaining, system watts, CPU load or download speed next to it.

How can I see which app is draining my MacBook battery?

Open MacMate’s Battery tab. Apps are ranked by real power draw in watts, with the percent per hour each one costs. Battery history shows the last 24 hours or 7 days, and MacMate can alert you when an app suddenly starts using a lot of energy.

Is there a free window manager for Mac like Windows snap?

Yes. MacMate snaps windows to halves, quarters and thirds with keyboard shortcuts or by dragging them to a screen edge, and its ⌥Tab switcher shows a thumbnail of every window, like Alt-Tab on Windows.

How do I reverse mouse scrolling on Mac without changing the trackpad?

Turn on “Reverse wheel direction” in MacMate’s mouse settings. It affects ordinary mice only, so the trackpad keeps natural scrolling.

Can I set a different volume for each app on Mac?

Yes. MacMate’s Sound tab has a volume slider for each app that plays audio, and switches the output device in one click.

How do I keep my Mac from sleeping?

Open Keep Awake and pick a duration, or choose “Until I Turn It Off”. You decide whether the display stays on too.

How do I completely uninstall an app on Mac, including leftover files?

Open Tools › Uninstall Apps, pick the app, review the files MacMate found and click Move to Trash. It can also find leftovers of apps you have already deleted.

Why does macOS say MacMate cannot be opened?

MacMate is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to MacMate. You only need to do this once.

Does MacMate work on Intel Macs?

No. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.

Is MacMate free and safe?

Yes. MacMate is free and open source under the MIT License. It has no accounts or analytics, its only network request is the check for updates, and its cleaners move files to the Trash instead of deleting them.
